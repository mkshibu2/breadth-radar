    About the Tool
    M.Radar (Market Radar) is a personal market diagnostics dashboard & screener designed to align your swing trading exposure with the market environment.

    For swing traders following price-and-volume breakout methodologies (such as Minervini, O'Neil, Darvas, and Weinstein), capital preservation and performance depend entirely on two core pillars:

    • Market Environment (Index Breadth): Evaluates daily Nifty 500 internals (A/D ratio, moving average breadth, 52-week highs/lows) to calculate a composite Breadth Score (0-100). This maps directly to specific Action Zones (Healthy, Expanding, Caution, Bearish), telling you when to aggressively scale up exposure or when to raise cash and stand aside.
    • Sector Analysis & Watchlist Sorting: Classifies 16 key sectors into **Weinstein Stages** (S1 Basing, S2 Advancing, S3 Topping, S4 Declining) to locate where institutions are buying. The drill-down lists display daily **VCP setup scanner candidates** with Scanner RS Ratings, and the **Breadth (BS) Sorting** feature automatically ranks watchlist stocks based on their parent sector's breadth strength first.

    By evaluating the market environment before entering setups and prioritizing stocks inside leading Stage 2 sectors, you filter out low-probability breakouts, trade with the wind at your back, and protect your capital.

    ⚡ 60-Second EOD Purchase Decision Checklist
    Top-down priority funnel for next-day swing trading decisions (O'Neil & Minervini)
    1️⃣ PRIORITY 1: The "Permission" Gate (O'Neil / IBD Core) Status & Analysis Strip

    Ask: Does the market permit me to buy? Stop immediately if conditions are hostile.

    Market Status Confirmed Uptrend = Green light
    Uptrend Under Pressure = Selective / 0.5R
    Rally Attempt = Wait for FTD
    Correction = NO TRADES
    Distribution Days (25d) 0–3 Days: Clean
    4–5 Days: Halve position sizes
    6+ Days: Freeze all new buys
    FTD Status Confirmed = Valid rally
    Voided = Low undercut, rally failed
    ⛔ RULE: If Market Status is Correction or Distribution Days are 6+, STOP HERE. Zero new buys tomorrow. Preserve cash.
    2️⃣ PRIORITY 2: The "Risk & Exposure" Dial Breadth Score & BSR

    Determine how much total capital to deploy and what risk unit (R) to assign per trade.

    Daily Breadth Score ≥ 55 (Healthy): 1–2R (75–100% exp)
    45–54 (Expanding): 1R (50–75% exp)
    35–44 (Caution): 0.5R (25–50% exp)
    20–34 (Defensive): 0.25R pilot only
    < 20 (Correction): 0R cash
    Breadth Momentum & Thrust Positive (+5 to +10): Expanding participation
    Negative: Narrowing breadth, tighten trailing stops
    BSR (Breakout Success Rate) ≥ 65%: Breakouts rewarded
    40–65%: Mixed, be selective
    < 40%: Breakouts failing in wild → halve position size
    3️⃣ PRIORITY 3: The "Sector Leadership" Compass Sector Command Tab

    Over 50% of a stock's gain is driven by its sector. Buy leaders in leading sectors only.

    RRG Quadrant Focus strictly on sectors in or heading toward Leading (green) or Improving (blue). Avoid Lagging.
    Weekly Heatmap Look for rows showing consecutive dark green blocks (outperformance vs Nifty 50).
    Sector % > 50 SMA Ensure sector has ≥ 50–60% stocks above 50 SMA. Avoid sectors where < 30% are above 50 SMA.
    4️⃣ PRIORITY 4: The "Stock Setup" Trigger VCP Screener

    Only execute if the individual stock meets the strict O'Neil / Minervini breakout criteria.

    VCP Grade & Score Prioritize Elite A+ or A candidates (Final Composite Score ≥ 80).
    RS Proxy Percentile Must be ≥ 80 (ideally ≥ 90) vs Nifty 500. Outperform 80-90% of all stocks.
    Pivot & Volume Dry-Up Stock resting within 2–3% of pivot with contraction-day volume below 50d average.
    ⚡ Quick 4-Question Nightly Execution Run
    1. Permission: Is Market Status in Confirmed Uptrend with < 5 Distribution Days? (If No → No new buys tomorrow.)
    2. Sizing: What does Breadth Score permit? (If < 35 → Max 0.25R pilot. If ≥ 45 → Normal 1R.)
    3. Reality Check: Is BSR ≥ 40%? (If No → Breakouts failing. Cut position size by half.)
    4. Stock Setup: Is candidate an A/A+ setup with RS ≥ 80 in a Leading sector? (If Yes → Place buy limit at pivot with 7-8% stop.)
